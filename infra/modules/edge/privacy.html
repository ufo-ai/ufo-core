ufo.ai ("ufo.ai", "we", "us", or "our") is operated by Metalcraft Inc. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our service.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your basic Google profile information: your name, email address, and profile picture (Google scopes userinfo.profile and userinfo.email). We do not request or receive access to Gmail, Drive, Calendar, or any other Google data.

When you use the service, we also collect usage information: the pages you open, the actions you take, your browser and device type, your IP address, and reports of errors you hit. This includes recordings of your sessions in the web portal. What you type into a field is masked in those recordings, and passwords are never recorded.

When you use ufo in Slack, we receive Slack messages, files, comments, profile information, metadata, and token data. This can include workspace, channel, thread, user, and app identifiers; names and email addresses; message text; files; reactions; timestamps; and authorization tokens. We receive this information when Slack sends an event or when ufo requests the context needed to answer.

Slack can include reactions and other fields that ufo does not use. We discard those unused fields after we process the Slack event.

2. How We Use Information

We use your Google profile information only to create and authenticate your account and to identify you within the service. We use usage information to operate the service, to find and fix faults, and to improve it.

We use Slack information to connect a Slack workspace to a ufo workspace, identify members, answer requests, keep conversation history, process files, secure the service, and provide support. We do not use Slack information for advertising or to contact Slack users outside the service.

3. AI Processing

ufo sends the Slack content and related context needed for a request to the model providers that process the request. We use this information only to provide and operate the service. We do not use Slack data to train an LLM.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy

Our use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy. We do not sell Google user data and do not use it for advertising.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it with the service providers and subprocessors that operate the service on our behalf, and only for that purpose. We also share it where the law requires.

6. Data Retention and Your Choices

We retain your information while the related ufo workspace is active and as needed to provide the service. We may retain information for longer when the law requires it.

To request access, transfer, correction, or deletion of your data, email support@ufo.ai. We verify that you have authority over the requested user or workspace data before we complete the request.

7. Children's Privacy

The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. ufo for Slack does not permit use by children under 16.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version on this page with a revised effective date.
