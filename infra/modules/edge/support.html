Support for ufo and the ufo Slack app is available without another account.

Contact

Email support@ufo.ai. We respond within two business days.

What to include

Include the ufo workspace name, the Slack workspace name, the time of the problem, and the error text. Do not send passwords, access tokens, or other secrets.

Privacy requests

Use the same email address to request access, transfer, correction, or deletion of your data. Read the Privacy Policy for details.
