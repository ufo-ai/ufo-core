A workspace holds one team, its agents, and everything they build. An agent reaches your accounts
through connections you approve in chat, remembers what matters across conversations, runs work on
a schedule, and hands back the files and sites it produces.

Nothing here is a settings screen you have to find. Ask the agent, and it carries the change out.
