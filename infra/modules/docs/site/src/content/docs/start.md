## Your first sign-in

The ufo team invites a new workspace by email. The invitation opens your workspace's first-run
page. It covers your whole email domain, so the person who opens it does not have to be the person
who received it, and there is nothing in it to retype.

1. Open the invitation and enter your work email.
2. Enter the code emailed to you, or choose Continue with Google and verify through your Google
   account instead.
3. The portal opens after verification. Your verified email domain opens the workspace.

A short setup confirms your website, then asks about your business, the roles you hold, the tools
those roles work in, and what is top of mind. It then offers connecting Slack. Only a workspace
admin can connect Slack; another member sees the step and is told an admin connects it.

An invitation works once per domain and lapses if it goes unused for a couple of weeks. Without
one, join the waitlist at https://ufo.ai

## Teammates join without an invitation

A teammate whose email domain already has a workspace signs in with their work email and joins it
directly. There is no invite code and no approval step, and the agent answers them straight away.

## Adding someone before their first sign-in

An admin adds a member by their work email — by asking the agent, optionally making them an admin
at the same time, or from the portal's Team view. Any email domain works, so a contractor or an
advisor is added the same way as a colleague.

The person is emailed that they were added, with a link to the ordinary sign-in page. Ask for a
silent add ("don't notify them") and no email goes out; the Team view carries the same choice as a
Notify toggle on the add form.

Where a verified address can enter more than one workspace, signing in asks which.
