---
title: Querying and analyzing data
description: Ask a precise business question and receive a reproducible analysis.
---

Connect the analytics service, spreadsheet, or other data source. Use read-only access unless the
task requires a specific write.

> Calculate weekly activated workspaces for the last 12 weeks. Use production analytics data.
> Define activation, show the query, include weekly counts, and report missing data. Do not write
> to the source.

## Define the question

State:

- The decision the result supports.
- The source, environment, and time range.
- Definitions for important terms.
- Required segments, comparisons, and exclusions.
- The output format.

If a term has several possible definitions, ask the agent to show them before it selects one.

## Require reproducible work

Ask for the query, formula, or transformation. The result must separate observed values from
assumptions. It must explain missing or filtered records. A chart must include the units, time zone,
and source.

Do not treat a failed query as evidence about product health. Confirm the connection, environment,
schema, and role first.
