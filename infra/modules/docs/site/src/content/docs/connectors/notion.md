title: Connecting Notion
description: Connect Notion to search pages and work with databases.
---

## Connect Notion

Ask the agent:

> Connect my Notion account.

Open the private authorization control. In Notion, choose the workspace and approve the pages that
ufo may access. Pages outside that selection remain unavailable.

## What ufo can do

- Search and read pages.
- Read database entries and properties.
- Create or update pages and database entries when requested.
- Use product notes, decisions, and plans in other work.

State the workspace area, page or database, and whether the agent may edit it.

> Review the launch plan in Notion. Compare it with completed GitHub work and list stale status
> claims. Do not edit the page.

## Use Notion as knowledge

Use a live connection for direct work. Use a [synced source](/work/sources/) when selected Notion
content must remain searchable as workspace knowledge or start tasks after changes.

## Fix Notion access

If a page is missing, open the Notion connection settings and confirm that the page is shared with
the integration. Reconnect when the grant expired or the selected workspace changed.
