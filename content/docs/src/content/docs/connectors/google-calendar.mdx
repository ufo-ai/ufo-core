Open the private authorization control and approve the correct Google account. Your calendar
permissions determine which calendars and events ufo can use.

## What ufo can do

- Search calendars and events.
- Read event details and attendee lists.
- Prepare meeting briefs from connected systems.
- Create or update events when you request a change.

State the calendar, time zone, date range, and whether changes are allowed.

> Review my customer meetings for next week in Pacific time. Prepare a brief for each from HubSpot
> and our meeting notes. Do not change the events.

## Fix Calendar access

If events are missing, confirm that the connected Google account can see the calendar and that the
request uses the correct time zone. Reconnect the account when Google removes or expires access.

See [Prepare a meeting brief](/recipes/meeting-brief/).
