## Connect Gmail

Ask the agent:

> Connect my Gmail account.

Open the private authorization control. Select the correct Google account and approve the access
shown by Google. The connection uses your Gmail permissions.

## What ufo can do

- Search messages and threads.
- Read message content and attachments needed for a task.
- Prepare or send email when the connected account and request allow it.

State the mailbox, time range, people, and action boundary.

> Find messages from Acme in the last 30 days. Summarize open requests and draft a reply. Do not
> send it.

## Fix Gmail access

Reconnect if Google reports that access expired or was removed. Confirm that you authorized the
account that holds the mail. Do not paste a password, authorization code, or message secret in
chat.

Connect [Google Calendar](/connectors/google-calendar/) or [Google Drive](/connectors/google-drive/)
separately when a task also needs those services.
