---
title: iMessage
description: Connect a phone and work with your UFO from iMessage.
---

An admin must make the workspace's first iMessage connection. Then each member can connect their
phone.

Ask your UFO to start the connection:

> Connect my phone to iMessage.

Complete the private connection step from your UFO. Do not send a code or credential in the chat.

Use iMessage for short requests and updates when you are away from the web portal. Use the web
portal for large files, long reports, task management, and workspace settings.

If your phone disconnects, ask your UFO to check the connection and start a new handoff.
