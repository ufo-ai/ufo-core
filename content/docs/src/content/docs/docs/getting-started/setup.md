## Create the workspace

The ufo team sends a workspace invitation by email. The invitation covers your work email domain.
The person who opens it does not have to be the person who received it.

1. Open the invitation and enter your work email.
2. Enter the code from your email, or select **Continue with Google**.
3. Confirm your company website, roles, tools, and current priorities.
4. Connect Slack, set up billing, or start a tour.

The invitation works once for its email domain. It expires after a short period if nobody uses it.
If your company has no invitation, join the waitlist at https://ufo.ai

## Join an existing workspace

Sign in with your work email. If its domain already has a workspace, you join it directly. You do
not need another invitation or an approval.

An admin can also add you by your exact email address before you sign in. This works for contractors
and advisers who use another email domain.

## Add your team

Ask your UFO to add a member:

> Add marshall@ufo.ai as a member and send the sign-in email.

An admin can add the member from the **Team** page instead. Turn off **Notify** for a silent add.
Members are not billed by seat. See [Members and admins](/docs/workspace/members/).

## Connect the tools for one task

Choose a first task. Connect only the accounts and knowledge that task needs.

A pull request review needs a [GitHub connection](/docs/connectors/github/). A weekly product report may
need your analytics service, issue tracker, and shared project notes.

Ask your UFO to start each connection:

> Connect my GitHub account so you can review pull requests.

Your UFO returns the correct authorization control. See [Connecting your systems](/docs/connectors/).

## Run the first task

Use one of the [recipes](/docs/recipes/) or write a request with a clear outcome, useful context, and a
definition of done.
