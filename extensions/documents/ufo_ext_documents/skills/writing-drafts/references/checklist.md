# Draft checklist

Run this against the finished draft, yours or an edited one, before delivering. Answer each check
pass or fail. Fix every failure and run the list again. Do not delegate this to a second agent. Check
it yourself.

For a detect request, check only the final section.

## Voice and meaning

1. Does the result preserve the member's point without adding claims, examples, stats, quotes, or
   opinions?
2. Does it preserve the writer's distinctive vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor, uncertainty,
   digressions, and level of polish?
3. Does it leave strong human sentences alone rather than rewriting them for consistency?
4. Is the cutting proportional to the actual slop, with no aggressive compression that strips
   character?
5. Would the writer recognize this as their own voice, and would it sound natural read aloud to a
   sharp colleague?

## Structure and sentences

1. Does it lead with what the reader needs while keeping personal setup that adds context, tension,
   or character?
2. Are points front-loaded where that helps, without forcing every unit into the same shape?
3. Do sentences earn their place, with concrete facts, protected specifics, and direct verbs?
4. Is it active voice with human subjects wherever possible?
5. Are genuinely tangled sentences fixed while clear spoken cadence, fragments, and changes of pace
   survive?
6. Is the original structure intact unless it was hurting the piece, with any reorganization
   explained in `What changed`?

## Words and patterns

1. Are banned words, filler phrases, empty adverbs, and inflated claims gone unless quoted as
   examples?
2. Are binary contrasts, negative listings, rhetorical setups, and throat-clearing openers removed?
3. Are faux-insight setups, colon reveals, superficial `-ing` analysis, fake-strong verbs, synonym
   cycling, dramatic fragments, and robotic rhythm fixed?
4. Are importance puffery and weasel attribution replaced with plain facts and named sources, or
   flagged to the member when no source exists?
5. Are fake-profound kickers deleted rather than rewritten into better metaphors?
6. Are summary-recap endings cut, so the piece ends on a concrete point, takeaway, or next action?
7. Is formatting slop gone: emoji headings, decorative mid-sentence bold, bullets that should be
   prose, headers over two-sentence sections?

## House rules

1. Zero em dashes, and no semicolon standing in for one.
2. No Markdown italics, no exclamation points, no emoji unless explicitly requested.
3. Sentence case after a colon unless grammar, a proper noun, a title, or code requires otherwise.
4. Headers plain, unnumbered, under six words.
5. Links carry descriptive anchor text, never bare URLs and never "source" or "link".
6. No "scrape" or "crawl" anywhere in the copy.
7. Every line's subject is the reader's gain rather than an internal mechanism, and no internal
   vocabulary reaches a member-facing line.

## Output

1. Does the delivery include the full draft plus a short `What changed` section?
2. For a detect request, does the response name each pattern with a quoted line and a short fix,
   without rewriting, scoring, or claiming AI authorship?
