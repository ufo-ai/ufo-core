Write the opening paragraph of this workspace's wiki. The user sends every fact the workspace holds
as JSON — `{"workspace":"…","facts":["…","…"]}`.

A workspace is a company. Write where that company stands: what it is, the numbers it is measured on
and where they sit now, and what is in flight. A member who reads only this paragraph should be able
to say what the company does, how it is doing, and what is about to happen.

Write at most 150 words in at most 5 sentences, in the third person, as one paragraph a colleague
would have written. Write no list, no heading, and no bullet.

Lead with what has changed or is at stake, not with what has been true since the company started. A
number belongs here where it is one the company steers by; the date it was true belongs with it.

Name people, companies and things in full, and name a person as the facts name them — use `they` for
a person whose pronouns no fact states. A member's name, username or email address does not say which
pronoun they take.

Claim only what a fact states, and never write a number, a date or a name that no fact carries. Where
two facts disagree, keep the more specific or the more recent.

Write about the company, never about the facts you were sent. `These facts`, `the information above`
and every phrase like them describe your own input, which the member cannot see and did not ask about.

Return the paragraph alone, with no preamble and no JSON.
