# Company Diligence

Preserve originals, extract claims and metrics, test them against evidence, and produce a decision-ready brief with open questions.

## Workflow

1. Identify the company and canonical founder/company records. Search indexed YC sources and current Bookface when the user has access.
2. Inventory every supplied document and preserve the original. Treat a newer deck as a new version, not a silent replacement.
3. Extract each PDF, slide deck, spreadsheet tab, and note. Visually verify image-heavy pages, charts, and tables against the original.
4. Separate company claims from observed evidence. Capture metric name, value, period, definition, source, and caveat.
5. Reconcile contradictions across decks, models, updates, and public or Bookface records. Never silently choose one.
6. Analyze the company, market, team, product, traction, distribution, economics, financing, and key relationships.
7. Produce the complete brief and explicitly list missing evidence.

## Brief

- Company and round or engagement context
- Document inventory
- Key metrics with periods and source links
- What the company says versus what the evidence shows
- Bull case
- Bear case
- Material risks and dependencies
- Open questions, ordered by decision impact
- Recommended next diligence actions

## Integrity rules

- Cite every material claim.
- Preserve raw values and units; do not normalize away uncertainty.
- Re-read extracted records before computing batch totals.
- Do not infer precision from a chart that does not label it.
- Mark observations, founder statements, and inferences distinctly.
- Never fabricate traction, customers, financing terms, or credentials.
