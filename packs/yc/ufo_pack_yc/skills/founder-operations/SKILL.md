# Founder Operations

Turn scattered updates into one measurable operating loop: evidence, primary metric, goal, inputs, risks, and next review.

## Workflow

1. Load current company evidence before asking questions: recent updates, metrics, meeting notes, pipeline, cash, and relevant indexed YC guidance.
2. Reconcile definitions. Separate live from contracted revenue, recurring revenue from run-rate, paid customers from pilots, and pipeline from traction.
3. Pick one primary metric for the company's stage and at most two secondary diagnostics.
4. Grade the prior goal red, yellow, or green from the actual number. Do not soften an ambiguous miss.
5. Set one roughly two-week goal in `metric: current → target` form. Reverse-engineer the customer, user, shipping, or outreach inputs required to hit it.
6. Tie the goal to runway, Demo Day, or the next financing milestone without making fundraising itself the operating goal.
7. Record owners, dates, and the next review. Use scheduled tasks only when the user asks for a recurring cadence.

## Investor updates

Preserve every raw source before extracting numbers. Parse deterministic fields first, use judgment only for narrative synthesis, then re-read the saved records before computing totals or trends. Deduplicate by company, reporting period, and source. Link each material metric to its source.

Use this structure:

- headline and primary metric change;
- wins with evidence;
- misses and causes;
- burn, cash, and runway when relevant;
- asks with a named recipient or introduction target;
- next period's one goal.

## Output

Return the operating decision first, then a compact scorecard and dated action list. Cite every number. Flag missing evidence instead of estimating it silently.
