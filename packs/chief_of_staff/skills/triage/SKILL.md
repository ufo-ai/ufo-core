# Triage — how a signal is weighed

Apply in order; earlier rules win.

1. **Rank for surfacing, never for filtering.** Ordering decides what the member reads first,
   never what they don't see. Every signal lands somewhere durable (an observation at least);
   drop nothing silently.
2. **Proximity weighs.** The same comment counts more from a direct report than from two levels
   away. Place a person with `graph_search` against the synced org chart before weighing them.
3. **Substance over ceremony.** Weigh what was said, not the length or seniority of the meeting it
   came from. Two Slack lines can outrank an hour-long review.
4. **Three independent sources make a pattern.** One mention of a risk is a data point. Before
   treating a concern as new, `memory_search` the theme; a second and third independent direction
   elevates it to act-on-now, and the proposal names the sources.
5. **Commitments outrank ideas.** A promise with an owner and a date becomes a follow-through
   check; a suggestion becomes a note.

A correction from the member is a rule candidate: restate it as a rule and route it through the
sync run's amend-the-manual step.
