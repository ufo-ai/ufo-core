# Automate operations

Ask only what remains unclear:

- the recurring workflow that costs the most time or causes the most failures
- its trigger, frequency, inputs, decisions, outputs, and owner
- the systems that hold each part of the record
- exceptions, approvals, and the cost of a wrong action

Map the current workflow before proposing a future one. Pick one workflow. Separate routine steps
the agent can complete from exceptional steps a person must approve. Keep an approval where money,
contracts, access, deletion, or an external commitment changes.

Plan one observed run, one bounded automation, and a comparison against the baseline before wider
use. A scheduled task can run a simple established instruction. Use an application when the job
needs durable instructions, its own access, repeated judgment, or a homepage.
