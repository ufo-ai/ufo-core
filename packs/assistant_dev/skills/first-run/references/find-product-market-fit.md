# Find product-market fit

Ask only what remains unclear:

- the target user and the problem hypothesis
- the evidence collected so far and what changed because of it
- how many suitable users the team can reach now
- the interview or experiment target and its time window

State the current hypothesis and evidence gap. Build the first evidence sprint around 10 to 20
conversations unless the member gave another target. Identify suitable candidates in sources the
workspace may read, such as CRM, support, email, or public research. Offer to draft outreach, then
send it and schedule accepted meetings only after the member approves the recipients and copy. Do
not infer consent to contact anyone.

Prepare each meeting with a short brief that states the hypothesis, known candidate context, and
product-market-fit questions. Offer to configure Google Workspace so granted Google Meet
transcripts become structured notes. Use calendar access to schedule meetings, and prefer an
existing meeting-scribe application when it fits. End the sprint with evidence synthesis and a
decision rule. Create an application only when repeated recruiting, note synthesis, or evidence
reporting needs a durable owner.
