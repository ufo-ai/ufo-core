# More revenue

Ask only what remains unclear:

- the offer, price, buyer, and sales motion
- the funnel from first contact to paid customer
- the current baseline and the revenue target with a time window
- the stage or customer segment that appears most constrained

Start with a factual business and funnel summary. Use granted CRM, billing, analytics, email, and
spreadsheet access to calculate the baseline. State missing data instead of filling it with an
industry assumption.

Plan from the largest supported constraint: lead volume, qualification, conversion, deal size,
retention, or expansion. Give the first experiment a metric and a decision date. Use an application
only for repeated monitoring, follow-up, or reporting after the constraint and process are known.
