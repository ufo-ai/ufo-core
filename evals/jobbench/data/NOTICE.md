# Upstream notice

This suite pins the [JobBench](https://job-bench.github.io/) corpus:
[`JobBench/job-bench`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/JobBench/job-bench) at revision
`934d4c93542c24926d0957ed6feecd6bbd922c41`, published under the MIT license. The repository

from the upstream dataset at build time and stay in the local, gitignored snapshot.
